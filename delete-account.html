Delete your data

Cozy Camp: Idle Island Life (com.aurelinteractive.cozycamp) · Last updated 2 October 2026

Cozy Camp has no accounts. You never sign in, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us or stored anywhere but your own device. So there is no account to close and nothing for us to delete on your behalf: you can erase everything yourself, at any time, for free.

Erase everything

  1. Open your phone's Settings and go to Apps.
  2. Choose Cozy Camp, then Storage.
  3. Tap Clear storage (on some phones, Clear data) and confirm.

All of your progress and settings are erased immediately. Uninstalling the game does the same.

Start over without erasing

In the game, Menu → Start a new game begins a fresh game. It keeps a backup copy of your previous progress on your device, in case you change your mind. That backup is removed when you clear the game's storage or uninstall it, as above.
