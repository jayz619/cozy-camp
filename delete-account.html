Delete your account

Cozy Camp: Idle Island Life (com.aurelinteractive.cozycamp) · Aurel Interactive · Last updated 2 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Cozy Camp: Idle Island Life account and the data stored with it. Both routes are free, and neither requires you to contact us first.

From inside the game

  1. Open Cozy Camp and tap Menu.
  2. Tap Account — if you are signed in, it shows your email address.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and everything synced to it are deleted immediately.

By email

Write to jason.crasto7@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject Cozy Camp account deletion. We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted, what is kept

DataWhat happensRetained for
Your email address, user ID and account name Deleted Immediately from within the game; within 30 days if you ask by email
Your synced game progress — your camp, regions, island, house and collections Deleted Immediately from within the game; within 30 days if you ask by email
Game progress stored on your device Kept. Deleting your account does not touch the copy on your phone — you can carry on playing without an account. To erase that too, clear the game's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the game Until you erase it or uninstall
Records of purchases — which product was bought and when Kept. Held by Google Play. We cannot delete these, as transaction records must be kept for billing and tax purposes Up to 7 years, as required by law
Analytics, crash-report and advertising data Not linked to your account. It is tied to your device's advertising ID or an app-instance ID. Resetting your advertising ID in Android's settings, or uninstalling the game, cuts that link Up to 14 months

No backup copy of your account is kept after deletion.

Subscriptions and purchases

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play, so cancel it there first: Google Play Store → your profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. One-time purchases such as Remove Ads belong to your Google Play account and can be restored from the game on the same Google account.

Deleting some data without closing your account

You do not have to delete your account to delete your data.

  • In the game — Menu → Start a new game begins a fresh game. Your account stays open.
  • By email — write to jason.crasto7@gmail.com and say which data you want removed. We will action it within 30 days and leave the rest of your account untouched.

If you never signed in

Cozy Camp does not require an account. If you have never signed in, there is no account, and your progress is stored only on your own device. To erase it, open your phone's Settings → Apps → Cozy Camp → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the game.
