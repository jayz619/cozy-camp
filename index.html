Cozy Camp

Idle Island Life · a cozy idle game for Android

A fog has rolled over the island, and it pulls back from firelight. Make camp, put the crew to work, and light the way from one clearing to the next. While they work, sail out to an island of your own and build it up one project at a time.

What's in it

  • An idle camp — your crew chop, gather and carry on their own, and keep earning while you're away.
  • An island to restore — the quarry, the riverside, the zoo, the farm, the old town and more, each with its own crew.
  • Coral Cay — the crew's own island: stairs, bridges and shops to build with what the camp earns.
  • Fishing and bug catching, with a museum to fill.
  • A house of your own — furniture, wallpaper and floors to arrange, and a garden that grows in real time.
  • Free to play, and playable offline. An account is optional.
