Privacy Policy

Cozy Camp: Idle Island Life (com.aurelinteractive.cozycamp) · Last updated 2 October 2026

Cozy Camp: Idle Island Life is an idle game for Android, published by Aurel Interactive. This policy explains exactly what the game collects, when, and why. The short version: the game is free and can be played without an account. Data leaves your device when ads are shown, when the game measures how it is being played or reports a crash, if you sign in, and if you buy something.

Accounts, ads and purchases are being introduced in stages, so the version you have may not include all of them yet. Where a feature is not in your version, the data described for it is not collected.

What we collect

Your game progress — your camp, the regions you have opened, your island and house, what you have found, and your settings Always, stored on your device So the game remembers where you are
Your email address and a user ID, and the name on the account you sign in with Only if you choose to create an account To identify your saved progress so it can follow you to another device

We never collect: your precise location, your contacts, your photos, your files, your microphone or camera, or anything you type outside the sign-in form. We never see or store card or payment details — purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

Accounts

An account is optional. If you create one, sign-in is provided by Google Firebase Authentication, and a copy of your progress is stored with Google Firebase so it can be restored on another device. We receive your email address, a user ID and the name on the account you sign in with; we never receive your password. You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time — see Deleting your data.

Ads

The free game shows advertisements, including optional rewarded ads that play only when you choose to watch one in exchange for something in the game. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To choose and measure them, AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, its IP address, information about how you interact with ads and the game, and diagnostic information about how the app is performing.

Location. We never ask for your location and the game cannot read it. However, an IP address indicates roughly where a device is — typically the city — so AdMob is able to work out an approximate location that way. Precise location is never collected. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it — including the EEA, the UK and Switzerland — you will be asked to choose what kind of ads you see before any are shown, and you can change that choice at any time from the game's menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

The one-time Remove Ads purchase switches off the ads that appear on their own. Rewarded ads, which only ever play when you ask for one, may remain available.

Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count things like how many people play, how far they get and which features are used. It works with an app-instance ID and, where available, the advertising ID, rather than your name or email address. We use it to improve the game and to see whether our own advertising for the game is working. See Google's privacy policy.

We also use it to see how many players come back on later days and how long they play, and to compare versions of the game: Firebase Remote Config and A/B Testing may give different players slightly different settings, such as prices or rewards inside the game, so we can see which works better. This uses the same app-instance ID.

Crash reports and performance

The game uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring. When the game crashes, a report is sent with what the game was doing at the time, your device model, operating system version, free memory and storage, and an installation ID. Performance Monitoring measures things like how long the game takes to start and how smoothly it runs. Neither includes your name or email address, and we use them only to find and fix problems.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game may remind you when something is ready, such as a full camp or a finished crop. Notifications sent from our side are delivered by Firebase Cloud Messaging, which uses a token that identifies the game on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings, and the game plays the same without them.

Purchases and the subscription

The game offers optional purchases: one-time purchases of in-game items, a one-time Remove Ads purchase, and an auto-renewing subscription that lets your camp keep earning for up to 24 hours while you are away. All of them are sold and billed by Google Play. We are told which product was bought, when, and whether a subscription is still active; we do not receive your card or payment details. See Google's privacy policy and our Terms for how purchases, renewals and refunds work.

Who your data goes to

Shared with an advertising business. Google AdMob receives your advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, how you interact with ads and the game, and diagnostic information, and uses them for advertising purposes, including its own.

Held on our behalf. These services process data to provide us a service:

  • Google Firebase — sign-in, storing your synced progress, analytics, crash and performance reports, game settings and tests, and notifications
  • Google Play — processing purchases and subscriptions

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires us to.

These services may process your data on servers outside the country you live in, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

Progress stored on your device stays until you erase it or uninstall the game. If you have an account, your email address, user ID and synced progress are kept until you delete the account, after which they are removed within 30 days. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, and crash reports for 90 days. Records of purchases are kept for as long as billing and tax law requires.

Deleting your data

Full instructions are on the delete your account page. In short, there are two ways and both are free:

  • In the game — if you are signed in, Menu → Account → Delete account deletes your account and everything synced with it. Clearing the game's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstalling the game, erases the copy on that device.
  • By email — write to jason.crasto7@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete your account and synced data within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your email address, your user ID and your synced progress. Records of purchases are held by Google Play under its own policy, and we are required to keep transaction records for billing and tax purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the data held about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to withdraw a consent you gave, or to object to how your data is used. Write to jason.crasto7@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Cozy Camp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and significant changes will be announced in the game.
