Privacy Policy

Cozy Camp: Idle Island Life (com.aurelinteractive.cozycamp) · Last updated 2 October 2026

Cozy Camp: Idle Island Life is an idle game for Android, published by Aurel Interactive. This policy explains exactly what the game collects, when, and why. The short version: the game plays entirely offline, has no accounts, shows no ads and sells nothing, and nothing you do in it leaves your device.

What we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Your game progress — your camp, the regions you have opened, your island and house, what you have found, and your settings Always, stored on your device only So the game remembers where you are

That is all. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

We never collect: your name, your email address, your location, your contacts, your photos or files, your advertising ID or any other device identifier. The game has no sign-in, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no advertising software in it.

Ads and purchases

The game shows no advertisements and has nothing to buy. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first, with a new date at the top, and the change will be described here before it reaches the game.

Who your data goes to

No one. We do not receive your data, so we cannot sell it or share it.

The game is distributed by Google Play. Google Play itself may record installs, updates and technical information about how apps run on your device, under Google's privacy policy and your own Google account settings. We do not control that and do not receive anything that identifies you from it.

How long we keep it

Your progress stays on your device until you erase it or uninstall the game. We hold no copy.

Deleting your data

Full instructions are on the delete your data page. In short: there is no account to delete, and everything the game has stored is on your own device.

  • On your device — in Android's Settings → Apps → Cozy Camp → Storage → Clear storage erases all of it. Uninstalling the game does the same.
  • Questions — write to jason.crasto7@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the data held about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, or to object to how it is used. We hold no data about you, but if you have a question or a request, write to jason.crasto7@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Cozy Camp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and significant changes will be announced in the game.
